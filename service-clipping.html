The problem

Long-form content is a goldmine most brands never mine

Every podcast episode, panel, livestream or interview a brand records has dozens of short, shareable moments buried inside it. Most of that footage gets posted once, if at all, and never turns into the volume of short-form content that actually moves an algorithm.

Marketplaces like clip-for-hire platforms solve this by crowdsourcing the editing to freelancers paid per view. That gets volume, but quality and brand voice vary wildly from clip to clip, since nobody on the other end has actually spent time with your brand.

What we do

One dedicated team, not a rented network

Swytch Studio runs Clipping as an in-house content practice, not a marketplace of freelance editors bidding on your footage. One team is assigned to your account, learns your voice, your platforms and your audience, and stays on it batch after batch.

How clipping helps you

Get in front of the audience that's already scrolling

Short-form has become the default way people discover new creators, brands and shows, hundreds of millions of people spend their downtime scrolling Reels, Shorts and Facebook. Clipping puts you in that feed without you having to become a full-time editor.

We start by agreeing on clear guidelines for how your content or brand should come across, tone, do-not-use moments, brand terms, then our team handles the cutting, captioning and posting from there.

FAQ

Common questions

What is clipping and how does it help?

Clipping means turning one piece of long-form content into multiple short vertical videos, then distributing them across Reels, Shorts and Facebook. Instead of one post and done, your content gets shown to a lot more people, a lot more times.

How is this different from running ads?

Ads are paid reach you rent for as long as the budget lasts. Clipping builds an organic library of short-form content that keeps earning views on its own, and compounds instead of resetting every time a campaign ends.

What happens if I don't have a lot of content?

We work with what you have. Even a single podcast episode or event recording usually holds more clippable moments than people expect. If you're starting from very little, we'll tell you honestly before taking on the engagement.

What exactly do you do?

We review your raw footage, cut and edit it into platform-native clips with hooks and captions, post it on a set schedule, and send you a weekly report on what's working.

Is the reach real?

Yes. We don't inflate numbers with bot views or engagement pods. What you see in the weekly report is what actually happened on the platforms.

How do you control quality?

The same dedicated team works your account every week, so hooks, pacing and brand voice stay consistent instead of resetting with a new freelancer each batch.

What is your pricing structure?

A flat monthly or quarterly retainer based on clip volume and platform count, not a per-view fee. See the Clipping Proposal for package details.

Sitting on hours of unclipped footage?

Send us your last few episodes or recordings. We'll show you what a week of clips would actually look like.
