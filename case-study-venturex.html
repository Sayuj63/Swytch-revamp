The challenge

A DMCC-based fund reinventing venture capital on-chain

VentureX Capital, a decentralised venture capital fund operating from DMCC, Dubai, aimed to modernise the traditional investment process through blockchain technology. The objective was to create an on-chain investment ecosystem where founders, investors, and the venture fund could interact transparently — while streamlining project discovery, funding workflows, and community participation.

Doing that meant re-thinking how a VC actually operates. Investment pools, project onboarding, LP relationships, and community governance had to be re-engineered around Web3 primitives rather than bolted on top of a traditional stack.

The approach

Web3 platform, product strategy and venture operations — one long-term partnership

Swytch Studio partnered with VentureX Capital as its technology and strategy partner, designing and developing the Web3 infrastructure that powered the fund's decentralised investment ecosystem. Our team built the technical framework for managing open investment pools, supported the venture's blockchain architecture, and facilitated the onboarding of promising Web3 startups seeking strategic investment.

Alongside technical execution, we worked closely with the leadership team on product strategy and ecosystem development — ensuring the platform, the deal pipeline and the community layer all evolved together.

A scalable foundation for decentralised venture investing

VentureX Capital established a scalable foundation for decentralised venture investing, supported by blockchain-powered infrastructure and a transparent investment framework. Through the combination of Web3 technology, product strategy, and venture operations, Swytch Studio helped create an ecosystem designed for the next generation of blockchain startups and investors.
