The challenge

A premium D2C brand without a premium ecommerce backbone

The GYG was growing fast as a D2C fashion brand, but their storefront couldn't do the product justice.

They needed high-quality product imagery, a scalable Shopify infrastructure, custom-coded features and a technically sound platform capable of supporting the next stage of growth.

One store launch had to solve for creative, tech and commerce at the same time.

The approach

Creative production and Shopify engineering, shipped as one build

We ran a dedicated ecommerce product shoot so the storefront had consistent, conversion-focused imagery from launch day.

The Shopify build was designed and coded around real product architecture — custom features, collection structures, navigation, payments and shipping — instead of a template's assumptions.

Everything was tuned for a smooth shopping experience across devices, and a foundation the brand can keep building on.
