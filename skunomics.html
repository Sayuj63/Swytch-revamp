What is your catalogue actually costing you before it even goes live?

Move the sliders to match your brand. This compares three real paths: traditional production, building an AI content pipeline in-house with your own team, and letting Swytch run AI imagery and video for you.

What "just use AI in-house" actually involves

01

Tool sprawl

A real pipeline needs a stack, not one app. Image gen, video gen, upscaling, editing, voice. Each with its own bill.

02

Someone has to own it

Prompting, re-generating, cleaning up hands and logos, picking the right output. That's a job, not a side task.

03

Consistency breaks fast

Without a creative system, lighting, skin tone, and framing drift SKU to SKU. Your catalogue stops looking like one brand.

04

Video is a different skill entirely

Motion, pacing, voiceover, and sound design don't work like static image prompts. Most in-house teams stall here.

05

The team's actual job stalls

Every hour in a prompt window is an hour not spent on merchandising, ads, or the customer.
