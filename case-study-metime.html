From zero to India's top family video platform.

Swytch built MeTime's entire brand identity, content system, and growth campaigns from scratch — taking the platform to 2M+ installs across India.

The challenge

Zero brand, zero audience, one giant category to break into

MeTime came to us with a product, a vision, and almost nothing else. No brand system, no audience, no content pipeline — and a family-video category already crowded with global streamers, regional OTTs and short-form giants.

The team needed a proposition that felt distinctly Indian, distinctly family, and distinctly not-just-another-OTT — and they needed it to translate into installs, not just applause.

Every part of the launch stack — identity, tone, content engine, paid, community — had to be built in parallel. There was no legacy playbook to lean on, and no time to sequence the work neatly.

The approach

One team building brand, content and growth at the same time

We assembled a single squad across brand, content and performance so nothing had to wait in a queue. Identity, story, editorial calendar and paid creative were designed off the same brief instead of being handed off between silos.

On the brand side, we built a warm, familial visual system — approachable typography, saturated but tender colours, family-first imagery — that let MeTime feel intimate at scale. It looked like a brand made by people who actually watch television with their families.

On the content side, we set up an always-on editorial engine: short-form clips, character-led series, cultural moments and hook-first thumbnails. Everything was engineered for the platforms where families discover apps — YouTube, Instagram Reels, and paid social — not just the app store.

On the growth side, performance media, creator seeding and community were run as one loop. Creative won on the feed, funnels converted the click, and community kept people coming back once they installed.

The outcome

2M+ installs, a real audience, and a brand that punches above its size

Inside 12 months, MeTime moved from a cold start to a household name inside its category — with the install base, community and brand equity of a platform much older than it actually is.
