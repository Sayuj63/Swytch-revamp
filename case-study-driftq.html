Driving innovation for AI-powered mobility, built for Indian roads.

DriftQ was launching an AI dashboard camera category into the Indian market. They needed a brand, a digital foundation and a marketing engine built for exactly that reality.

The challenge

A new product category, built specifically for Indian driving

DriftQ was entering a fast-moving auto-tech category with a product tailored to Indian roads and driving conditions.

That meant more than a launch — it meant educating a market on why AI dash cams matter, why theirs is different, and why now.

The brand needed clarity on positioning, a digital foundation to earn trust, and a marketing plan that could actually convert curiosity into orders.

The approach

Brand, tech and marketing built as one launch stack

We shaped brand strategy from the ground up — positioning, voice, visual system — then built the website against it, not around a theme.

Tech consulting and creative production ran alongside brand and site work, so nothing shipped in silos.

The launch marketing engine was designed to do two jobs at once: introduce a new category, and convert educated buyers into customers on the same funnel.

The outcome

A cohesive launch across brand, tech and growth

DriftQ went to market with a full brand, a conversion-focused digital presence and an integrated launch marketing stack — a foundation built for long-term growth, not just launch week.
