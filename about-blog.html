AI & Creative

AI Fake Ads and Content Creation Redefine Brand Storytelling

RS
Raj Shah
Chief Business Officer, Swytch Studio
📅 Nov 12, 2024
⏱ 6 min read

For decades, producing a brand film meant a real barrier. A TVC required a director, crew, studio, talent, and a budget running into lakhs. That barrier is gone.

The democratisation of cinematic production

AI-generated content tools have reached a quality level where, for many applications — product showcases, lifestyle campaigns, social content — the output is indistinguishable from traditional footage, at a fraction of the cost.

"The brands that figure out AI content production in 2024 will have a 3-year head start on everyone who waits for 'the right time' to start."

At Swytch, we've embedded AI into creative production since early 2023 — not as an experiment, as infrastructure. Every brand we work with has some part of their content pipeline powered by AI: fashion shoots, CGI renders, or AI-assisted scripting.

What this means for D2C brands

The implications for D2C brands are significant. A brand that couldn't afford a monthly campaign video can now produce one at a fraction of the cost. A fashion brand that needed a studio and models for every SKU can now generate visuals in 48 hours.

This isn't the future of marketing — it's the present. And the brands adopting it now are already seeing the results.
